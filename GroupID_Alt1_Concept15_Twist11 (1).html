Ancient focus

Book II of the Arthashastra sets out the akshapatala (records-and-audit office, AS 2.7) and a catalogue of roughly forty ways government servants could embezzle revenue (AS 2.8).

Modern parallel

Reconciliation, audit evidence, authorization controls and fraud investigation across five different fraud typologies.

Artifact application

Each case isolates a different embezzlement pattern — unapproved payment, fictitious vendor, ghost employee, fake credit note, unsupported reimbursement — and tests the same investigative discipline on each.
